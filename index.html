Lucy: From Kenya to Germany 🇰🇪🇩🇪

One journey, two worlds, countless memories.

My name is Lucy Wambui, and my journey began in Kenya 🇰🇪. Like many young people, I had dreams that extended beyond the places I had always known.

In September 2026, I took a big step and moved to Germany 🇩🇪. Leaving home, family, friends and everything familiar was not easy, but I knew that growth sometimes requires courage.

Germany has introduced me to a completely different way of life. I am learning German, discovering new cultures, making memories and learning to become more independent.

But this journey is about more than travelling. I am also working towards building a career in IT. I am a beginner in coding, learning how websites work and discovering the exciting world of technology.

My dream is to combine my education, creativity and new technical skills to create opportunities for myself. Every small project, every new German word and every challenge is another step forward.

From Kenya to Germany, and wherever life takes me next, this is my story. I am still learning, still growing and just getting started. ✨
